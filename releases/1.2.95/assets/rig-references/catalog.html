The WoW lion is now a separate Wildbound enemy

Dreadmane Lion uses NPC 55491, display 39134, model 124781. Its skeleton and animation tracks were read directly from the public viewer's M2 and eight external animation files; no GLB export or installation was needed.

69 source bones become 37 native editable joints. All 49 sequence entries are retained, including 11 aliases: 38 distinct sequences. Wildbound draws its own pixel body, mane, skull, eyes, ears, muzzle, nose and jaw.

The new encounter, The Dreadmane wakes, adds a left-right claw combo, a telegraphed area roar and a crouch-to-pounce attack. Sleep and wake clips are also used. Existing lions and wolves are preserved.
