# April and the villagers

Original native pixel artwork based on the two user-supplied October 7 references. No source character mesh, skin, or baked whole-character frames are used. `src/villager-data.mjs` holds Fern, Bram, Poppy, Milo, Theo, Wren and April's presets, names and dialogue. `src/villager-motion.mjs` retargets the existing Captain motion into the villager's proportions without replacing the Captain or base human.

April's October 7 reference refinement uses broader asymmetric auburn locks around the cheeks and shoulders, tall blue eyes with white glints, a warm face and toothy smile, a folded sleeveless green scarf top, pale rolled boot cuffs and a wider silver blade with its gold guard. These are April-specific native part changes; the six other villagers and all authored joints, animation keys, part overrides, directional visibility and equipment fits remain intact. The supplied reference is `codex-clipboard-b6606536-30f8-42ca-9538-6413b4b8c67b.png`; preview sheets are review samples, not imported runtime frames.

34 joints, eight directions and 36 clips: 31 retargeted Captain actions plus native wave, cheer, shrug, sword-ready and jumping-swing gestures. Shorter limbs, a longer readable torso, more hip separation and a larger head-to-body ratio fit the supplied pixel style. Separate face, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, hair, shirt, trousers, sleeves, hands, boots and sword render through the same native part capture/override pipeline as Rig Studio. Independent villager palette fields and a Studio variant picker let the artist preview and recolor each member. April's colors use the main palette fields. Shapes, keys, bone sprites, directional visibility, layer order and sword fitting remain editable.

“Seven faces by the fire” appends at event index 73. Its seven civilians are unattackable, have short stories and harmless gesture interactions, and never enter hostile combat collections. A story counts when the player selects its story reply. Seven different stories recruit April for that player. Progress is independent in multiplayer; only one April joins at a time. She follows on the surface, through storage/temple/underground rooms and across Wildbound destination changes. Her owner’s death ends the friendship; revival does not award her again for the same camp. JSON expedition saves preserve story progress and the active follower.

`Meet Villagers.cmd` opens an isolated, muted actual-game camp test. `Play Villagers Test.cmd` previews the cast and clips. `scripts/verify-villagers.cjs` checks all poses, independent parts, actual Studio, mouse dialogue, portraits, event spawning, recruitment, Game.update following, save restore and death. Pass `--build <packaged-game-folder>` to verify packaged resources. `qa.json` records the checks; `cast.png` and eight-direction action sheets are assembled previews, not runtime sprites. Visuals are a review sample.
