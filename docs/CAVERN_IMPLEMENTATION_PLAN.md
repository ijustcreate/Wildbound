# Cavern reference reset and implementation plan

The user rejected the native cavern study. The seven newly supplied images now define the visual standard. The two generated concepts are visual targets, not implemented game screenshots or approved shipping assets. The second concept has clearer pixel clusters; both still require adaptation from their isometric composition to Wildbound's existing camera.

References: the top-down ruins for gameplay readability; the branch, waterfall, dock, snowy arch and bridge images for sculpted forms, grouped pixel detail and depth; the dark fantasy scene for selective teal light. Copy their visual principles, not their characters or architecture. Keep floors calmer than walls and crystal highlights. Exact generation prompts are in `art/caverns/prompts.json`.

## Staged implementation

1. **One playable art sample — ready for user review.** The opt-in native slice now has a connected chamber and bent side passage, a shallow/deep underground pool with an irregular shoreline, crystals, and native cavern props. It uses the actual game Renderer, player animator, lighting and HUD. The sample was checked for player-radius connection, spawn clearance, water depth states, mining and shelf support. Review `test-output/cavern-room-slice/gameplay.png` with the matching room sketch `test-output/cavern-room-slice/overview.png` before expanding the catalog or full room network.
2. **Editable geological layers.** Keep floor material, solid-rock mask, wall faces/caps, foreground overhangs, water and placed props separate. Derive navigation and rendered edges from the same chamber outlines and passage control points. Use concave pockets and varied wall heights, not uniform outlines around round rooms. Near-camera rock needs cutaway/fade behavior that keeps the player and hazard tells visible.
3. **Small native asset kit.** Resolve a tall wall section with inside/outside corners, limestone shelf, asymmetric stalagmites, wall-grown crystal vein and mineable base, rubble, pale fungi, and drip/pool edges. Store editable indexed pixels or native source geometry alongside independent anchors, footprints, height, occlusion and harvest state. Respect custom sprite overrides. Do not bake the generated scene into a background.
4. **Gameplay integration.** Exterior rock blocks traversal. Water behavior and visible shoreline share one surface definition. Low shelves have real jump support; columns remain solid. Crystal mining updates artwork, loot, light and saved depletion state. Decorative veins and reachable mining targets must read differently. Ceiling hazards remain sparse: dust and growing shadow, escape time, one small hit, persisted spent state. Foreground art must never hide their warnings. Any raised route shown in art must have corresponding traversal geometry.
5. **Animation, light and ecology.** Reuse the budgeted cached lighting and shadow systems; animate drips, ripples and restrained crystal pulses separately from static assets. Use the existing cave cricket, blind fish and peaceful olm habitats. Check both cavern events in the new room layouts. Treat the concept waterfall as optional decoration, not an assumption of new vertical traversal mechanics.
6. **User review before expansion.** Show normal gameplay, an editor collision overlay and movement behind a foreground wall. Correct silhouette, scale, lighting and readability against the references. After visual approval, expand to branching rooms, loops, dead ends and varied formations. Extend the approved rules into Boundless with matching entrances and persistent region state.
7. **Verification and one release.** Test actual player-radius reachability, water transitions, shelf landing, mining/light removal, hazard escape/hit, wildlife, events and save/reload. Verify Studio terrain paint, prop movement/resizing, custom pixels and collision edits. Render positive, negative, far and seam Boundless regions. Run the full suite and muted packaged Electron checks after integration, then coordinate one desktop build.

## Acceptance

Geological depth comes from sculpted wall masses and overlapping planes, not border rings. Gameplay floor and player remain readable at normal scale. Crystals grow visibly from fissures; water has distinct shallow margins. Materials use coherent stepped pixel clusters. Interactive objects, lights and collision remain independently editable. Existing 1.2.36 runtime, saves and approved swamp art are unchanged during this concept/planning phase.
