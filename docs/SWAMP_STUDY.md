Native art prompt: compose one quiet bayou clearing around a winding deep channel with uneven shallow shelves. Keep open ground readable. Group reeds and lilies by shoreline habitat, leave open stretches of water, and use one deliberate narrow crossing. Draw tall, irregular old cypress with buttressed trunks and a different low, spreading mangrove with open stilt roots.
