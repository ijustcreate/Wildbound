# Wildbound 1.0.63 polish pass

Preserves normal Electron launch/profile, existing authored rigs and uncommitted work. No Git push or Windows-security changes.

## UI/gameplay

- Inventory item wells no longer inherit thick action-button nine-slices. Larger item art, legible equipment labels, all eleven equipment slots and twenty-four bag slots; empty footer/actions collapse, tooltip remains floating.
- Grounded hunter/released followers leave footprints; spectral and flying followers do not. Water remains print-free.
- Per-enemy perception excludes diving players except crocodiles/alligators, and excludes players beneath bridges/quicksand for everyone. Specialized gorilla/spider targeting uses the filtered targets too.
- Event reveal cannot reduce party framing zoom more than 15%; does not center the camera on a distant event spawn.
- TV world is a finite 4,800-unit level, one board at its center, party starts nearby. Actual shared board art/dice pose renders above it during a frozen roll sequence. Random bounded goomba spawning; one-use question blocks release a collectible purple-quality Strange mushroom (unknown use). Items enter the owning normal inventory; a full bag leaves loot in place. Board progress/physics remain separate from the normal expedition.
- Three cached parallax woodland layers reuse normal forest pixel-art trees rather than flat vector trees. Spell bolts use cached colored cores, short luminous trails and sparks, with fallback while textures load.

## Rendering/performance

Three zoom tiers: full at zoom >=1, medium >=.55, distant below .55. Scenery cache <=24 MiB, enemy pose cache <=128 entries (max ~8 MiB), tinted particle cache <=128 96px images (~4.5 MiB). Players/active attacks remain full detail. Small decorative ground detail density and particle count reduce with tier. Existing terrain chunks/culling remain; this is not a rewrite of every subsystem or a larger-world-coordinate migration.

Transient particle emitters cap at 48. Gameplay drawing budgets 384/256/128 by tier. Editor can deliberately preview all eighteen recipes with a separate 768-draw budget. Deterministic typed-array plans avoid particle-object allocation per frame. No full-screen blur or per-frame texture generation.

Software-rendered 18-effect stress showcase measured median CPU submission ~0.8–1.4ms with p95 spikes ~43–87ms. These are diagnostic software-renderer submission costs, NOT real-game FPS or a promise of no slowdown. Hardware/controller and very-large-level playtesting still needed. RAM reduces repeated artwork generation but cannot eliminate simulation/draw cost. Million-blade grass should use GPU instancing, chunk culling, shader wind, a small player-interaction field, and baked distant grass—not one Canvas actor per blade.

Prior 1.0.62 broad smoke reported sixteen unresolved checks (some old UI expectations, some Field Kit/editor/opening checks). Do not claim those are all fixed or mark this public-release certified. Targeted source/package checks and final test count are recorded in chat; normal user saves were not used for testing.
