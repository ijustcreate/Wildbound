## Changes

- Archers under close pressure get at most a short 0.35-second reposition followed by a roughly two-second planted approach window. Obstructed sight routes toward the player instead of indefinite strafing. The shared offensive admission and projectile quiet windows remain authoritative.
- Target-aware arrow retrieval yields to close pressure, ignores arrows embedded in scenery, and limits a distant search to two seconds before a six-second retry delay. Empty ordinary/tactical archers can close for a telegraphed physical bow bash instead of idling or conjuring ammunition. Native punch clips visualize anticipation, delayed contact and recovery; no authored rig edits.
- Watchers and constructs use the normal obstacle/door navigation service during approach. A Game method supplies this service to creature packs without introducing a rig/definitions import cycle. Watchers hold position at close range while waiting for a group turn. Watcher/book windups keep a target id; losing that target gives recovery rather than snapping to a new co-op player.
- Books retain their three-paper volley and recovery; their physical page swipe is short-range rather than an invisible 185-unit melee hit. The volley no longer creates a generic floor ring. Existing native paper projectiles and body animation provide the cue. Shared bronze/teal pixel footprints are preserved.
- Local same-kind packs assign stable primitive role slots: direct hunter; lead targeting four 32-unit ground tiles ahead of observed movement; interceptor taking a shorter lead plus side approach; and flanker. Roles affect navigation only, never attack aim, damage or simultaneous-attack limits. Prediction stops for stationary players, collapses to direct approach within melee distance with hysteresis, falls back when blocked and is disabled for committed attacks, bosses, allies/pets and non-player goals. A surviving member takes over direct pursuit after hunter death; new arrivals fill free slots without reshuffling surviving slots. Wolves retain their alpha/target and bite scheduling while gaining distinct pursuit approaches. Other normal navigation, night-enemy approach and creature-pack approach routes use the same goals.
