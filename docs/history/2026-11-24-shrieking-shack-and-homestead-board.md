# Shrieking Shack and Homestead board

Remus Lupin appears outside the Shrieking Shack. The Wild Lands clock changes him into the native werewolf at midnight. He patrols the grounds, hunts nearby monsters and players, and reverts to an injured person when defeated. The encounter does not award a kill or a werewolf drop. If players leave him alone, he walks back into the Shack at dawn. His encounter checkpoint lives with the other Hogsmeade state.

Lupin teaches the Patronus Charm when either hand has a wand equipped. Practising beside human Lupin or striking a hostile ghost or Happiness Devourer with a wand summons the learned form for 30 seconds. The companion pursues spirits near its owner and can return on another qualified cast after fading. The form is saved per explorer, while the active summon is temporary. Asking Lupin to relearn excludes the previous animal from the next roll. The blue material recolors each creature’s normal rig and uses bounded pixel glints.

The Shack wardrobe can release a large spider, Happiness Devourer, werewolf, Death Eater, or harmless bunny. Hostile Boggarts award Dark Essence instead of their ordinary drops. A cabinet cooldown prevents stacking encounters. The visible floor hatch accepts Interact or a nearby click and displays “Seems this passage is locked for now.”

The physical Homestead board uses the Classic roll and win rules in the active Wild Lands session. Interact near the table to close up and play. On a portal roll, only the roller shrinks into the board and reappears at a dry destination. The remaining explorers stay at the table. There is a chance of one encounter outside the Homestead. A teleported explorer must walk back to the house before their next roll. Mid-effect checkpoints retain the chosen destination and award the teleport once.

## The two pirate ships

Captain Hook, Smee, and the crew have routes and conversations aboard the outdoor ship. Endorsing Peter Pan in Hook’s conversation makes the fighting crew hostile. Hook drops his coat, hat, hook, and Fancy Rapier. Pirates can drop a reusable Scarlet Parrot Cage. The parrot matches the tiny barn owl's scale and keeps its name when recalled. The Jolly Roger flies at the main mast, where the Lazy Pirate remains in the crow’s nest. Tick-Tock is a threefold-sized crocodile with native swim and jaw phases and positional ticking.

Mystery Inc. meets explorers outside Smugglers’ Hollow to investigate One-Eyed Willy’s treasure. Falling stalactites telegraph before striking the cave paths. Tricorn-wearing cutlass skeletons guard the separate ship’s top deck. Follow the hatch and hold passage into Willy’s treasure room. Inspecting the seated skeleton awards one equippable eyepatch per explorer. His size was reduced and the interaction caption moved below the player so the chair remains visible.

## Weapons and weather

The Veilkeeper’s Chest in the Shadow Realm guarantees the five-bladed Glaive. Equip it in either hand. A throw seeks visible nearby enemies, with extra range, speed, and sparks at full charge. The other hand's weapon can attack while it is in flight. The Wise Little Goblin in the Bayou hut gives one green energy blade through dialogue. It has a thin glow, movement and swing trails, positional sound cues, and localized scorch marks.

The northern half of the Overlook maze uses snowy paths and a mix of fully and partially covered hedge tops. The rough transition blends into the green south half. Snow falls in the snowy maze and Frostmere. Forest and Bayou each roll independently at 20% per daylight and night period. The 120-second storms ramp up and fade down. Per-view precipitation fades during a biome crossing, so a split-screen player elsewhere sees their local weather.

## Alley and Hogsmeade updates

The Diagon Alley and Nocturne Alley street widths are 30% narrower. The layout keeps shop dimensions, usable doors, and the paths through the garden and behind Ollivanders. The Three Broomsticks has a snow-covered exterior, seated guests, casks and a mounted boar head. Bring a wand in hand or in the backpack to enter. Rosmerta sells Butterbeer and shares a rumor about the Shrieking Shack. Floo Powder opens a destination list at the inn, Wild Lands Homestead and Nocturne fireplaces. The player spends a pinch after committing to a clear arrival. Within the same loaded area, only the traveler moves. Crossing between loaded areas follows the shared-screen party travel rules.

Zonko’s sells a Whizz-Bang with a rising trail and aerial burst, Flaming Footprints that leave temporary flames and small grass scorch marks, and Shrinking Candy. Each candy lasts 60 seconds. The shrinking effect halves character size and physical damage. Spell damage keeps its normal value.

## Borgin’s mirror and the garden pond

Stand facing Borgin’s mirror for thirty uninterrupted seconds. The skeletal reflection steps out as a hostile enemy. The first defeat drops an owner-named Stolen Heart and clears the reflection. A later visit can start another fight with a heartless reflection. Any explorer can pick up a drop. The heart’s ownership follows the explorer through storage and saves. Borgin offers 50 Dark Essence for the owner's heart through an extra dialogue choice.

Jump into the Mage Garden pond with a Stolen Heart to restore it. The carrier can return their own or another explorer’s. The pond takes the item, resets the owner's mirror, and gives the owner a one-minute cosmetic glow. A return for an absent owner is checkpointed for their next visit to the same expedition. Luna Lovegood tells the pond legend from its east bank.

## Train boarding and resident activity

Players board the train by jumping into an open side doorway. Ground-level movement stops at the train, including under the raised door. Passing the threshold requires a jump or broom flight at floor height. A small correction helps line up a jump close to a door while the player steers inward. The crossing preserves the player's position at the threshold. Both coach sides and broom entry passed muted Electron checks in the Wild Lands. George Weasley and Lee Jordan carry brooms over one shoulder on their existing Diagon Alley walking routes. The carry pose follows the native rig's hand and gait across its eight facings.

## Coastal camps and Wild Lands encounters

Ten beach sites have small Merlock camps with fires, driftwood shelter and fish racks. Two to five Merlocks share a camp and return after pursuing explorers. Wolf groups spawn together in packs of two to five. A five-member pack has one visibly larger alpha and uses the native pack combat. Habitat-colored slimes appear across the Wild Lands. The encounters share the normal spawn budget and persist through expedition checkpoints.
