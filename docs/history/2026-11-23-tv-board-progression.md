# TV board progression and readable hazards

User reports: the board could not be hit again, chasing thorns had no interactable source, the highest masonry was not walkable, the exit door was unwanted, and tube outlines/UI were confusing.

The TV world now advances its shared 48-space board on every successful roll. Living creatures, queued spawns and uncollected challenge seeds do not lock subsequent rolls. Held buttons still cannot reroll, and the short board reveal consumes held inputs. Reaching 48 wins after the reveal and grants the existing shared treasure chest. No exit door or right-edge finish trigger remains. The chest and victory camera now stay by the central board. Clearing a challenge offers an optional blessing via Up + Interact at the board rather than forcing every player into a choice screen.

Thornstorm uses two visible native-pixel Briar Hearts on the middle side ledges. Each grows fixed, telegraphed lanes near its own roots. Strike or shoot it twice, or Interact nearby to uproot it; destroying the heart removes its thorn lanes immediately. No player-following timer remains.

The highest masonry on both sides has a matching one-way collision surface. Crown ledges are offset from the tier below to preserve headroom, and question blocks were moved out of their jump approaches. Hold Jump and steer outward from the upper tier to reach them. Down + Jump drops through them like other one-way platforms. Tube pair identification uses a plain colored bar; rune text and pairing outlines are removed. Arrival warnings remain distinct from pair identification.

Builtin image generation created `assets/tv-arena/journey-board-v1.png`. Its exact prompt is in `design/tv-arena/parallax/journey-board-prompt.json`. The 512×256 nearest-neighbor cached plate is used by a live side-view board with 49 spaces, animated shared marker and live progress, and by the 'Warning: The game is real' entrance. Board paths, numbers and markers are rendered live, not baked into the background. A persistent progress ribbon and encounter/next-roll prompts explain the loop.
