# Wildbound 1.2.92 — mode generation and train traversal corrections

- Root cause: lobby setWildboundMode wrote generatedEnvironment before generating a map. Game.start trusted that marker and reused forest geometry for House, or house geometry for Classic Forest. Mode selection now changes only the requested environment. Fresh starts also repair the two known mislabeled/missing-geometry cases. Lobby previews still update immediately. Native tests cover Classic Forest -> Wildbound -> actual furnished House -> Classic Forest -> actual native grass renderer.
- Train inventory previously skipped all rider transport updates. Carrying now runs before modal input handling, including seated orientation and gangway interpolation. Character controls stop while inventory is open; the train, riders and shared camera keep moving. Co-op test opens every rider inventory simultaneously.
- Walking outward through either end doorway now leaves the train without Interact. An airborne side exit also works; a short reboarding cooldown prevents being pulled straight back on. Ordinary standing walls/interior obstacles stay solid.
- Carriage crossings use continuous progress over a physical, rendered gangway between the current endpoints, including curves/elevation and direction reversal. The player no longer snaps from one interior anchor to another. Roofs account for both connected carriages during crossing.
- Open compartment doors slide their full panel beside the doorway. Renderer and collider share the same panel geometry; no thin pole remains in the center.
- Train prompts use compact outlined sans-serif captions at screen resolution, matching lobby prop typography, with per-player keyboard/controller labels and short door/seat/cocoa/cab actions. Inventory hides contextual captions.
- Station cats are increased from 26 to 34 render units. Persistent roaming actors follow reachable waypoint routes around room walls, furniture, luggage and water, accelerate/decelerate, turn gradually, advance gait from distance and pause/sit/sleep between walks. Drawing no longer moves their world position with a sine wave. Authored cat anchors remain editable.

Verification before packaging: 38 muted Electron selector/map-generation checks; 58 muted native train/editor/renderer checks; focused regression tests cover repaired worlds, inventory carry, walk/jump exits, reverse gangways, sliding-panel collision and two minutes of bounded cat motion. Final full rebuild, package hash/boot/launcher and HTTP web checks are recorded in test-output and delivery receipts. No hardware controller, remote multiplayer or FPS claim.

Normal-profile launcher: Play Wildbound.cmd. Authored rigs, personal saves and unfinished house/railway drafts are preserved. Source identity is the commit containing this note; build manifests record it with sourceDirty=false. Source push and versioned Pages deployment are separate; original and previous web releases remain intact.
