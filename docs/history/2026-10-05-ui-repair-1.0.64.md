# Wildbound 1.0.64 — storage, inventory and input repair

Normal Electron profile and all existing characters/storage are preserved. No Git commit/push or save migration. Build is from modified source on 1891708d014594bd837f1a7049ecefa04d16dae4.

## Changes

- Inventory: quiet item wells, larger icons, empty-slot silhouettes, compact action footer and distinct input badges. Salvage is visible/usable in the lobby; yields and socketed trinkets go directly to the real backpack there. Insufficient space rejects the operation atomically. Expedition salvage still leaves manual-pickup drops.
- Private/shared chests: chest and backpack are visible simultaneously with 24 slots each, including empty cells. Single click selects, explicit button or A transfers, double click transfers, shoulders/Tab switch containers. Shared overflow is paged. Item/socket metadata and permissions remain intact. Victory/ritual loot retain separate popups.
- Vending: animated machine preview and 12 purchase cards no longer overlap. Separate name, icon, price and remaining stock; collection tray remains available. New expeditions reset stock; reopening and restoring a saved expedition do not. Gold and paid pending orders are not reset.
- Pause: explicit gold/bright selection handles gamepad programmatic focus, independent of browser focus-visible heuristics. Directional navigation follows columns and volume pairs. Start/keyboard pause records its explorer; the controller confirming Dev menu or entering the pause cheat updates the item target to that explorer.
- Developer items: separate Give to my inventory and Spawn on ground buttons. Owner is refreshed on every opening. Quantity is validated (1–999) and bulk inventory grants are atomic. Full-backpack failures are reported, not falsely acknowledged. Ground spawns require normal manual pickup; in a private room they appear outside the storage portal, as the notice explains. Successful grants persist.
- Built-in oak, birch, pine, snowy pine and palm roots have tapered silhouettes. Snow is a collection of irregular shaded drifts. Roots/stump stay stationary while only the upper trunk/canopy pivots at the cut; fall accelerates before settling. Interactive falling props bypass the zoom cache.
- Whip: fixed 18-segment rope buffer, coiled rest/wind-up, delayed traveling curve, extension, brief tip crack and recovery. Model-space projection preserves directional views. No persistent particle emitter is created; reach/damage are unchanged.
