# Wildbound 1.0.74 — accumulated changes, healing, old-well tunnels and storage jumping

## Scope and preservation

Includes the entire accumulated checkout and the [1.0.72 changes](2026-10-06-ui-coast-spiders-1.0.72.md), plus healing magic, an old-well encounter, honeycomb, tooltip-only loot details, new lobby prop art and storage jumping. The base before integration was `edc664ea35a4c75710570680dca26c6e1387f658`. The user requested a GitHub push and a new versioned desktop/web build. Authored rigs, saves, the running 1.0.73 game and its packaged directory are preserved. The new launcher uses the normal save profile, with no `--preview`.

## New work

- Light-blue healing wand: ally-only projectiles, effective green healing numbers and rising blue pixel particles. Halo head gear boosts incoming/outgoing spell healing. Wand + halo adds independent once-per-second healing for three seconds, capped at three stacks; capped hits do not keep refreshing old stacks. Hostiles, PvP opponents and dead actors are never healed or damaged by a healing shot. The healing projectile path is isolated from ordinary damaging spells.
- Old well: an appended encounter available in forest and house grounds, preserving previous event indices. A solid stone well with rope/bucket leads to a native pixel underground tunnel network with exploration fog, spiders, bats and persistent treasure. Room-local state lives on its portal and survives JSON save/reload. Interacting at the daylight shaft/rope returns to safe ground in the original expedition; no board or level reset. Storage portals remain separate.
- A destroyed beehive drops three honeycombs exactly once. Each restores up to 25 health from the backpack. Full-health/dead characters do not waste a comb; effective healing numbers do not report overheal.
- Loot names, descriptions, stats and robot prices use item-slot tooltips. Removed duplicate below-grid detail panels from inventory, victory/starter chest and robot. Operational feedback stays in a compact status region. Controller selection updates the tooltip; mouse hover/focus also works. Grid/action space is reclaimed without hiding icons or buttons.
- New native lobby map table, recessed dice tray, carved difficulty totem and explorer station art. Wood/brass/felt/parchment materials, biome maps and difficulty tiers have distinct pixel details. Footprints, interactions and shifted-right difficulty position remain unchanged.
- Storage portals now have a stepped pixel rim, two inward-rotating spiral arms, a dark throat and 28 analytically animated square particles instead of a large blurred ellipse. Their work and particle count are bounded. The outside owner can press Portal again to close: empty rooms disappear immediately; occupied rooms use the existing ten-second grace and forced-exit/damage rules. Repeated presses do not restart that countdown. Owner reentry still cancels collapse; normal guest exits are safe. Lobby and expedition paths are covered.
- Calling Wildbound at the finish table has priority over loose loot and embedded arrows, even with a full backpack. Holding Interact completes the call without attempting to collect/equip loot. A short/aborted call does not collect loot on release. Loot interaction away from the finish table is unchanged, and nearby loot prompts no longer hide the finisher interaction.
- SCRAP-9's repair is character-owned, retained through profile capture/assignment, new expeditions and session reload; guest repairs apply to the storage owner, not every player. First interaction shows a compact owner-input dialogue explaining repairs, item sales, equal-price buyback and persistent chest storage. The introduction is remembered per character. Already repaired legacy characters skip it and stay repaired. The Continue button is visibly selected and has keyboard/Xbox/Switch labels.
- Inventory character visibility: the character was rasterizing, but an older `position:static!important` rule painted it behind the absolute background canvas. The scoped foreground canvas now stays absolutely positioned above that background, below the rotation controls. The equipped preview remains animated, rotates independently and fits small/co-op panels.
- Storage jumping: owner-routed jump input works in both expeditions and lobby rooms. Room-local height/velocity keeps the outside actor, furniture collisions, depth order, shadows and station locations on the floor. The shared humanoid takeoff/air/fall/landing poses lift visibly. Held input does not auto-repeat or double-jump; release/press permits another jump. Airborne station/automatic-exit interactions wait for landing; explicit portal return resets room height. Pausing freezes room movement.
