# Wild Lands and alley follow-up

## Desktop controller and saved explorers

The Xbox picker gave its character carousel the default focus. A fresh Confirm could advance the preview without assigning the explorer. Cancel could also leave an unspawned slot behind, and a delayed desktop controller claim could swallow the first fresh Confirm. The default action now selects the visible explorer, cancel removes an unspawned slot, and the title only waits for release when the join button is still down. The muted renderer verified all 13 paths, including actual Animator movement, a delayed claim and co-op ownership, using simulated Gamepad API input. The physical Xbox controller was not used by the automated test.

The controller hotfix is build `1.2.133`, from modified source at `5b244635ab78e5a3c8e78ef21fdbabf60e505fd7` on `Wildbound_worlds`. The normal-profile Desktop shortcut `Wildbound Worlds (1.2.133).lnk` pins `Play Wildbound.cmd -BuildDirectory "dist\1.2.133\Wildbound-win32-x64"`. The package booted in muted smoke mode with no errors. No player profile was changed during verification.

## World changes

- Ticket clerks have individual names and conductor caps. An empty express costs 5 gold and stops after ten seconds. An occupied train reports its station and direction without charging. D-pad Up casts storage, Right toggles loot names, and Left opens the native game board in World and Wild Lands. Mounted broom riders use open coach doorways with solid body panels around them.
- All lobby Wilds entries now start on a clear Homestead floor with the selected profiles, gear, and gold. The fresh expedition uses a separate checkpoint. The old Wilds checkpoint and its backup remain available as files. Native light was sampled in day, at night, and on returning to daylight.
- Smaller Wild Lands grave markers and plots use snapped native pixels. Melee leaves walkable rubble and preserves partial damage. Nightglass has a separately operated rear door and iron gate. Each named grave can be read, and each newly broken headstone rolls a 25% chance to raise a hostile ghost.
- The Bayou uses one dedicated editable otter rig for play and art tools. The lower silhouette uses short rounded limbs and a tapered tail. Back-floating and eating loop without a tail discontinuity. The exact rejected barrel sprite could not be matched to a recovered runtime path. The dormant generic otter painter was removed and unrelated actors were retained.
- The new Hogsmeade interiors sit north of Frostmere. The Three Broomsticks, Zonko’s and the boarded Shack have 43 individually placed props between them. The Shack planks break first. The exposed door can then be opened with Interact or broken by a second round of attacks.
- Alley work includes the walkable Ollivanders passage, additional façades, directional signs, a narrower Nocturne connector with a north wall, lightly animated fog and street grit, light on players and props, and a purple Diagon portal with brick wings. The counter hat display at Nocturne Curios was removed, the shared Floo hearth uses animated fire and embers, Griphook opens a dialogue for exchanging five Dark Essence for fifty gold, and named Hedwig renders 10% smaller.
- Nocturne’s black cat runs toward an eligible nearby wild rat. The rat uses a reachable wall hole, waits about 20 seconds, and reappears at a different route point. The cat stays in the alley and resumes its patrol.
- Celia gives the broken-mirror clue to the Lamplit Vault. Brindle sells two ordinary drinks. A real player knockout in the ring permanently unlocks his 24-gold Champion’s Draught. One dose gives +15% character size and outgoing damage for 90 gameplay seconds. Another dose refreshes the same effect.
- Bellglass offers 48 zodiac readings, a reusable 35-gold crystal ball with 20 distinct yes/no replies, and 12-gold Magic Mushrooms. Each portion adds one minute, with a three-minute cap. The GPU pass bends and recolors the gameplay view, adds neon edges and bright trails, and fades down as each minute expires. It reuses the original arena and HUD input. A Canvas fallback runs when WebGL is unavailable.
- Wrong Turn Door & Key sells 24-gold mystery keys. Interact spends one key to open an individual door. Walking through makes one random choice among 13 categories. The railway-station category includes all eight stops. Cross-layer travel uses the native party transition. The same-layer Curios destination moves just the traveler. The bed landing uses real rest, and the coach outcome lands on a real moving train.

Verification media remains in ignored `test-output/` folders. The focused source and package checks use isolated profiles and muted Electron windows.

## Rails, coast and Greywake

- The conductor offers every station and runs the train to the selected platform at setting 2, 96 px/s in the Wild Lands. Taking the cab controls cancels the destination and keeps the existing speed. Native braking reaches the authored marker and three whistles sound at 1.8-second intervals. In the cab, the D-pad and left stick operate the throttle and the compact caption includes Leave Controls. The whistle is about 4.3 dB louder than its previous mix.
- The locomotive boiler is around 10.5% narrower. Rear cab views draw the lit iron firebox over the boiler. A seated explorer’s pelvis is aligned to the cushion, and the seat matte preserves the legs and shoes in visible viewpoints.
- Pixel grass overlaps both riverbanks. Tall cattails use rooted, wind-driven stems and brown seed heads. Bayou ground cover uses the native movement-responsive grass shader at a shorter height. Ocean and river water use periodic shader noise and a single clipped native surface fill. Train silhouettes, the railway fascia and submerged piers enter the water reflection pass. Companion owls hover above water and find dry footing on shore.
- Wild Lands beaches and seas support native gulls, pelicans, catchable crabs and reef fish. The fish validate deep coastal water. Gulls and pelicans remain airborne as they cross the shore. The eastern peninsula uses continuous sand instead of rectangular Classic terrain cells.
- Hostile Wild Lands crabs use the native pincer attack sequence with distinct raised, pinching and recovering claw poses. The damage lands during the visible pinch. Passive catchable crabs retain their separate behavior.
- Greywake has separate upper and lower render layers. The opposite elevation softens, and the summit rim covers a lower character without masking a character on the summit. The lower south coast is walkable sand up to the entrance of Smugglers’ Hollow, with wading shallows offshore. Native checks covered both camera elevations, summit foot positions, and the cave route.

## Tideglass Reach

The new eastern coast connects to the mainland on foot above Greywake. Mara Sounding’s survey camp introduces the trail around the lagoon, the Singing Stacks, the Drowned Orrery and Windward Arch. Activating the three stones lights Stormwatch and unlocks its lockbox. The Tideglass Lens fits native gear sockets and grants +12 maximum mana and +2 mana/sec. Opened stones use the expedition checkpoint. The lens is awarded once per explorer, and a full backpack leaves the claim available.

Muted native checks walked the entire signal route, awarded and verified the lens, and checked checkpoint restoration. The crab replay fired a real hit in the pinch phase and showed the claws in all eight facings. The earlier ice and swamp dice-roll regression reproduces in packaged 1.2.133 as well as this source. The ordinary Wild Lands encounter and coastal wildlife checks ran independently of that legacy dice path.
