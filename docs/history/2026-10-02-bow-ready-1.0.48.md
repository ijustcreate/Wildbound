# Bow ready pose — 1.0.48

User reference: relaxed archer with the bow diagonally across the lower torso, a resting arrow, broader planted stance, and clear grip. Replaces the old upright side carry.

- Idle/walk/run bow hands are posed together in front of the torso, with a slightly wider idle stance and IK arm fitting.
- Carried bow geometry rotates across eight directions with a minimum side-on width for readability. Aiming retains separate upright geometry.
- Curved, outlined wooden limbs replace the two straight V-shaped segments. Fingers render over the grip.
- Relaxed pose shows an arrow only when the selected ammunition is present.
- Bow layer ordering is constrained relative to the torso, including saved rig orders, to avoid a rear-facing bow floating over the back.

This improves the pose and equipment rendering using the existing pixel model. It is not a complete higher-resolution character-art replacement or a claim of matching all detail in the reference image.

Preview: `scripts/verify-quiver.cjs` also renders the equipped bow in 56 facing/action combinations in isolated Electron. Regression tests are in `tests/appearance.test.mjs` and `tests/visual-feedback.test.mjs`.

Known unrelated issue: the previously packaged 1.0.47 full Electron smoke suite reported 14 failures (several editor interactions, lobby portrait, gameplay assertions and Field Kit). This bow change does not claim to repair those failures. Saves and authored rig files are preserved.
