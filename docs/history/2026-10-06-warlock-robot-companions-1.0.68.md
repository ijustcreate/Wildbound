# 1.0.68 — humanoid warlock, robot buyback, victory chest and companions

## Scope and preservation

The warlock's legacy creature art, oversized victory chest, missing shop cursor, sparse robot sales pages and poorly animated friendly creatures were reported together. This release includes the uncommitted 1.0.67 inventory work; neither turn has been committed or pushed. Base commit: `edc664ea35a4c75710570680dca26c6e1387f658` on `main`, tracking `origin/main`. Personal saves, shared authored rigs, earlier builds and other running games were preserved.

## Changes

- Purple warlock now uses the sculpted generation-three humanoid rig with fitted plum cowl, split ritual robe, bone mantle, gloves, boots, vestment, sigil and articulated skull staff. It walks, channels summons/healing, recoils and dies through that rig. Old warlocks carrying `staff` receive the new visual equipment without mutating their gameplay gear or rewards. Costume items are excluded from random gear rolls and have distinct icons. Animation Studio/project rig packs can edit and persist the warlock separately; older packs remain valid.
- Robot panel uses the exact inventory footprint (maximum 480 × 800 CSS pixels, responsive co-op placement), portrait above, six compact buyback slots and every one of the 24 backpack slots below. No sparse item pages or category filters hide inventory. Item names, stack totals, gold, rarity strips and a thick inset gold cursor remain live. Sell, buyback and split are separate buttons on one row. All previous robot stock is accessible through six-item buyback pages, at the original sale value. Transfers retain sockets/bag contents, fail atomically when full or unaffordable, and cannot repeat a completed purchase. Shared-stock changes normalize the remaining cursor without changing another player's selection.
- Normal and TV victory chests are capped at 440 × 520 CSS pixels and use six columns/four rows. Normal private/shared chests retain their four-column, two-container layouts. D-pad movement matches the victory grid. Selected-item information stays beneath the slots; detailed tooltips are hover/focus-only rather than automatically covering the compact chest. Restart/lobby actions and the TV shared-loot-before-return rule remain intact. Extra compact styling covers 420px-tall windows.
- Friendship conversion clears pinned editor frames and stale hostile phases, including old saved allies. Both friendship allies and Ranger/ritual pets track actual displacement: feet advance with travelled distance, slow follow uses walk, fast travel uses run, fliers use their flight clip, and stopped/blocked actors return to idle. Hit, bite, casting, jumping, sit/roost, death and explicit editor previews retain priority. Catch-up teleports do not become a giant gait step. Collars use the same animated pose, and ghosts' footprint exemption is unchanged.
