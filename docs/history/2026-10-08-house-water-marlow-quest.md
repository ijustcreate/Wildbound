# October 8, 2026: house recovery, water combat, Marlow, and tracker

The reported house screenshot was the five-room original built-in floorplan. The normal desktop profile's saved `default` design was byte-for-byte the original built-in layout, while its unfinished House Builder draft differed by one wall height. The source has a newer six-room gallery floorplan, but the saved library continued selecting the old one. On load, only an exact fingerprint of the original built-in `default` is upgraded; the prior version is archived as a selectable design and the unfinished draft remains unchanged. Custom layouts do not match the fingerprint and are not migrated. The user's normal profile was inspected read-only; no live save was overwritten during this work. If the intended layout was a different custom design made on another computer or browser origin, it still needs to be located and imported separately.

Glass breaking now uses the bundled heavy-glass recording for both one-hit panes and structural window cells. Snakes and anacondas can move through deep water; surface swimmers remain targetable, while submerged players remain hidden. Ordinary snakes cancel their attack when the target dives, while anaconda bite timers retain their existing pause behavior. Floating player corpses and swimming snakes are clipped at the waterline, bob subtly, and draw surface ripples. The mystery objective tracker is a compact upper-right panel instead of a wide central overlay.

Marlow Moss now renders through the shared editable human rig with a dedicated hat, vest, straps, gloves, half-cloak, trousers and boots; the donkey remains separate. The previous merchant actor lacked `sprite:'player'`, so only its separately drawn hat appeared.
