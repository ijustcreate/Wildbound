# Spider, boar and elephant motion transfer

The existing Wildbound spider, boar and elephant now use native editable pixel rigs driven by selected World of Warcraft bone tracks. Reference pages: [Spider display 35985](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=106469/spider#modelviewer:1:35985:0), [boar pet family](https://www.wowhead.com/pet=5/boar), and [Trunks display 61148](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=88830/trunks#modelviewer). The boar page is a family, so classic boar display 193 was selected as a representative donor. No WoW mesh or texture is used at runtime. Source M2 and metadata are kept under `assets/*-wow` as import references and excluded from the desktop package.

The importer `scripts/import-wow-creatures.cjs` generates `src/wow-creature-motion-data.mjs`. `scripts/register-wow-creatures.mjs` updates only the spider, boar and elephant entries of the shared authored rig file. Stored custom art and clips are preserved during legacy migration. The spider's eight legs retain grounded walk contacts and a tucked death pose; the boar gains a bristled silhouette and softened lunge. The elephant has a new native part renderer (`src/elephant-pixels.mjs`) with a single full-bodied torso, fitted head, lower floppy ears, curved tusks and tapered trunk, and separate hoofed feet. Eighteen parts are captured by Studio for editing. The sandy tarantula, rhinoceros and other creatures are unchanged.

Focused Node checks (37/37) and muted Electron checks cover the authored rigs, full clips, eight facings, real game boot and Studio selection. Contact sheets and the QA report are in ignored `test-output/wow-creatures/`. The guarded authored-rig comparison allows only `beastMotions` and `nightMotions`. The long rebuild suite was interrupted during art revision; its one center-finish failure passed twice when rerun in focused tests. The final art then passed source and packaged Electron QA. Concurrent workspace work advanced the shared package version, so the verified desktop build is `1.0.113-merchant-center`, made from modified source at commit `bc1b432`. Its six creature source/authored files match the app ASAR byte-for-byte. No commit or push was made. Launch through `Play Wildbound.cmd` or the versioned Desktop shortcut in the normal save profile.
