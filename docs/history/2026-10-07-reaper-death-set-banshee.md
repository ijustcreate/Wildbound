# Reaper, Death set and Banshee skills — 1.0.93-death-frost

Reaper events can occur in every zone, with four times the selection weight in the cemetery. The enemy uses the editable humanoid rig, custom hood/skull, ribbed chest, skeletal gloves, robe skirt, Death's Shroud cape and scythe. Scythe sweep/reap and one-handed raised casting are native editable animations.

At half health or below, the Reaper can drain a living player, monster or critter. Each half-second removes 5% of the victim's maximum health and heals the actual health taken. A completed five-second channel kills the victim and fully heals the Reaper, as originally requested. Hitting the Reaper interrupts the channel; completion and interruption enforce an 18-second cooldown. The beam connects raised left hand to target chest, with endpoint particles and returning soul motes.

The six-piece Death set includes the scythe. Three pieces leave bounded black walking particles, also emitted by the enemy. All six add a glowing scythe blade, 20 scythe damage and 5% actual-damage life steal.

Banshee Queen retreats from nearby players with a collision-aware dash and three-arrow spread on an eight-second cooldown. After two seconds at a safe distance she can hold a stationary 1.8-second frost shot on a twelve-second cooldown. A successful fully charged arrow freezes for three seconds; friendly player melee or arrows break the ice without friendly damage. Blocked/invulnerable hits do not freeze. Damage interrupts charging.
