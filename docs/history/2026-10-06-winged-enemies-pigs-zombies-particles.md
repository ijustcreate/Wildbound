## Implemented behavior

- **A kiss with thorns**, appended event index 55: two equipped humanoid succubi, 180 HP / speed 56 / damage 18 each. Optional wing and tail bones belong to the variant only. Walk, low hover, whip, kiss, hurt and death clips use native pixel art. Slow heart kisses have a one-second warning; shield, dodge, walls and jumping avoid them. Charm lasts five simulation seconds, shows hearts/countdown and temporarily controls the player against uncharmed teammates even with ordinary PvP disabled. Succubi prefer uncharmed humans within 520 units and protect pets while those targets are nearby; otherwise they attack the pets. Owner death, friendship, player death, rooms, expiry and victory release control. A two-second grace prevents immediate re-charm. Released projectiles retain their original team.
- Each succubus drops `succubus_horns` (head), `succubus_whip` (hand1) and `succubus_wings` (shoulders) once. Custom icons and fitted art. Player wings are cosmetic: no automatic tail, new default humanoid bones or flight ability.
- **Three sparks of trouble**, appended index 56: three small red/bone-horned flying imps, 60 HP / speed 70 / damage 14. A 0.9-second glowing-hand windup locks a landing marker. Fireballs travel along a real height arc, collide with geometry and leave radius-30 ground circles for 3.2 seconds. Water fizzles them. Ground burn checks every 0.45 seconds; jumping clears flames. Bombs/fire are plain saved records capped at 24/16; detonation visuals use the existing transient particle cap, not persistent particle actors. Befriending the owner clears its hostile hazards. Flight is visual; feet/collision/depth stay on the ground plane, with a smaller ground shadow.
- **Footsteps without a heartbeat**, appended index 57: three slow humanoid zombies, 80 HP / speed 32 / damage 14, including shambler, missing-left-arm and broken-jaw variants. New stooped idle/shamble/claw clips, torn clothing, decayed face, claws and jaw art. A 120-degree forward cone and line of sight acquire only human players. Stationary range is 180; movement extends it to 360. Rear approaches do not trigger an omniscient turn or hearing exception. Claws warn for 0.65 seconds, hit once and recover for 1.25 seconds. Shared humanoid drawing changes are variant-conditional, not applied to every player.
- Pink `pig` and brown saddle-spotted `pig_spotted` are neutral, zero-damage wildlife in forest, Jungle Temple and Beach. Both are approachable unarmed and flee held weapons/injury like deer. New articulated eight-facing pixel art has four-leg trot, floppy ears, snuffling head, blinking eyes, curly tail, faster flee gait and collapsed death pose. Deer families remain; total ambient wildlife cap is eight.
- **Black-hole vortex**, `px-black-hole` / `pixel-black-hole`: original time-sampled black core, ragged gold-white ring, spiral embers and cool orbiting flecks, selectable/saveable in Particle Engine. Source-over preserves the dark center. It loops and uses existing emitter/draw/quality limits. Sixteen baked phases and a hard 64-texture cache ceiling (at most 1 MiB of RGBA raster storage). Reduced motion freezes the internal composition. The editor's gallery/count/page labels are now derived from preset totals so the new 37th pixel preset does not hide the last page. This is an editor/effect-engine preset, not an unrequested damaging spell.
- New custom humanoid AI routes explicitly retain capture, interrupt, slow-trap, freeze and once-only defeat/reward behavior. Interrupt cancels pending attacks and holds the same recovery timing as other enemies. Saves preserve variants, windups, charm timers and imp hazards without duplicating actors/rewards.
