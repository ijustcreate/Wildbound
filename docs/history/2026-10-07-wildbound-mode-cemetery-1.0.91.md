# Wildbound mode and cemetery art — 1.0.91

Wildbound mode is the first selectable card in the map menu, with the same checkmark/selected treatment as the environment cards. It is not an on/off switch: selecting it keeps it selected, and selecting an ordinary map or Boundless leaves it. The last choice persists; fresh installations default to Wildbound. Open party menus update together, and readiness is invalidated on selection changes.

The campaign starts in the house. Dice totals 5 and 8 replace the ordinary encounter with one of six destination verses (Forest, Ice, Desert, Jungle Temple, Beach, Cemetery). A bag visits every destination before repeating. In an away map, either total returns everyone home. Normal rolls, doubles and turn order retain their normal rules. Players, their current health/inventory/quests/pets, and all board progress carry forward. World arrays, terrain, doors, loot, encounters, clocks and ambient life pause independently. Cached worlds move by reference rather than deep-cloning every trip; saved snapshots omit players and nested campaigns from inactive maps.

Maps prepare in a module worker during the roll reveal. The renderer warms two bounded scene images during that reveal, then animates cached explorer sprites into/out of the board over a 1.4-second departure and 1-second arrival. World simulation is frozen during travel. A failed worker leaves the current world intact and allows a later retry. Save/reload resumes either transition stage. Completing the game away from home uses the existing level-sealing effect, restores the saved house, clears its hostiles and exposes the normal shared victory chest. Starting another expedition clears old travel snapshots.

Generated cemetery board and map-card PNGs use the shipped board and map illustrations as references; exact prompts and provenance are in `assets/CEMETERY_ART.md`. Board path coordinates and the center well remain aligned. Cemetery cats flee from nearby aimed attack attempts, steer around obstacles, settle at their new location, and stay neutral/uncatchable. Their saved flee state survives reloads. The running pose uses the existing native pixel palette.
