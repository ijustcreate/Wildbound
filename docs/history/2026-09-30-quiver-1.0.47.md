# Quiver rendering — 1.0.47

- Replaced the fixed screen-space backpack offset and square item-icon rendering with a narrow, elongated quiver attached to the posed chest/pelvis/shoulder frame.
- Rear offset is projected with the character facing, so leaning and fallen poses keep the quiver attached. Equipped arrow decoration reflects available ammunition; empty quivers no longer inherit the inventory icon's permanently drawn arrows.
- The quiver layer explicitly stays behind or in front of the torso/cape according to the projected back direction. Saved rig orders cannot append it as a universal foreground overlay. Other authored layer order remains unchanged.
- The inventory/loot quiver icon is also narrower and taller.
- `tests/appearance.test.mjs` exercises ten actions, eight facings and three animation times with a saved layer-order override. `scripts/verify-quiver.cjs` renders an isolated Electron contact sheet to ignored `test-output/quiver-preview.png`; 56 poses were visually checked.

This build also includes the unpushed 1.0.46 lobby TV changes. No character saves or authored rig files were replaced.
