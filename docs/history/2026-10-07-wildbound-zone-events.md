# Wildbound arrivals and zone-weighted encounters — 1.0.94-zone-events

First arrival in a fresh Wildbound world now starts an encounter from that world's available event pool as the travel transition completes. Each destination has encounter-weight preferences so the forest leans into jungle wildlife, the temple into its guardians, the desert into burrowers and heat, the ice into frost creatures, the beach into water and shore encounters, and the house into its own shared events. Events remain available across multiple zones when appropriate. Existing environment locks, time gates, cemetery restrictions, boss pacing and event-history coverage remain in force. Revisiting an explored map and returning to the house preserves that world's saved encounter.
