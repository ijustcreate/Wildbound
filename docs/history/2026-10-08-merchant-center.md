# Merchant ledger and center-board finish — 1.0.113-merchant-center

Marlow's trading UI now uses a single wood/brass frame, a portrait rendered from his existing character rig, readable parchment item rows, native item icons, separate Buy / Sell / Buy Back categories, and an explicit confirmation button. Selecting an item does not spend money. Details and owned quantities are inside the shop, without floating item tooltips. Empty slots and single-page navigation are removed. Buyback lists only the original owner's items; sparse backpack navigation skips holes and retains item metadata and prices. The gold balance and player name have separate space from the close button.

When two or more merchant menus are open, player panels share the full viewport in a grid rather than reserving a wide gameplay corridor. Compact panels put the description in the selected row at 13px, with the trade action and controls outside the scrollable list. Larger solo panels retain the separate illustrated detail pane. Two-player 600×431, four-player 1280×800 and six-player 1600×900 layouts were inspected in muted Electron. Lists scroll for further stock; text is not shrunk to fit every item at once. Ordinary inventory docking is unchanged unless multiple trading panels require the shared layout.

Landing at progress 48 now marks the roll as a center landing, displays WILDBOUND (including a readable label below the resting pawns), and preserves the existing event instead of spawning another or triggering 5/8 world travel. Finishing pieces are skipped in subsequent turn order, including saved games, while unfinished players continue rolling. Any living finisher beside the board can hold Interact (E, default Xbox Y / Switch X) to call the existing finale, independently of whose turn is next. The hold takes priority over pickup aliases and automatic nearby loot; a dedicated loot press without the hold still works. Opening a menu, entering a room, death, stun or charm cancels accumulated hold progress. Calls cannot interrupt an active dice sequence.
