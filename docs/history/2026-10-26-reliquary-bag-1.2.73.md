# Reliquary Bag bonuses and Use transfers — 1.2.73

Reliquary Bags previously suppressed the bonuses of their contents. Carried bags now contribute the same passive relic stats as loose backpack relics, without counting them twice. Only direct relic contents count; nested bags and non-relic trinkets/equipment do not become passive equipment. Chest storage remains inactive. The bag tooltip lists aggregated numeric bonuses and resistances, its slot count and Use instructions. Empty bags show no bonuses; bags outside the player's backpack label the totals as bonuses when carried. Tooltip content refreshes when bag contents change, including repeated transfers.

Use (and the inventory toolbar's Equip alias) on a backpack relic stores it in the first carried Reliquary Bag with capacity. A full first bag is skipped. No bag/full bags leave the relic untouched and give clear guidance. Protected relics remain protected. Inside an open bag, Use takes the selected item back to the backpack only if there is room. Existing mouse bag-item clicks and drag/store controls remain. Transfers preserve metadata and stats, refresh vitals and persist.
