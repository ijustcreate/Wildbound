User requested a rebuilt side-view Wildbound TV level following four supplied forest-ruin references, a central board that summons enemies, linked tubes, player/monster spawn points and original Wildbound mushroom/turtle enemies with editable eight-direction animation. The previous course was a seed-shuffled flat forest strip populated by unrelated expedition creatures and timed ambient goombas.

## Changes

- Hand-authored, bounded 2,880-unit arena; the board is exactly at x=1,440. Mirrored lower and upper jump routes, central wooden bridge, crown platform, mossy side vaults, visible spawn alcoves, fern/torch props, layered forest/aqueduct/waterfall backdrop and sun shafts/motes.
- Three tube pairs: A/B connect lower and upper routes, C crosses the upper arena. Interact at an opening; a protected transition moves the player to its linked lip. Held input/cooldown prevents immediate return. Four hearths support checkpoints and elevation-correct respawn. Up/down do not add depth motion.
- Board strikes require the board’s actual ground elevation (upper-floor attacks cannot reach it) and retain the shared dice reveal and queue waves at six alcoves, spanning ground and upper platforms. Only Bramblecaps and Mossbacks appear. Occupied alcoves defer independently. Active plus queued enemies are capped at 24; no timed ambient spawning occurs.
- Bramblecaps patrol and can be stomped or fought. Mossbacks retract on stomp, can be kicked by contact/strike, ricochet at masonry/bounds and defeat enemies with the sliding shell. Idle shells wake. Emergence, retraction, waking, hit, contact attack and defeat drive native animation clips.
- Both new creatures have native rigs, eight directional drawings and idle/walk/run/attack/hurt/death/jump/emerge/retract/shell/slide/wake clips. Directional pixel pieces attach to their own editable joints; rear eyes/head are hidden appropriately and withdrawn limbs/head cannot reappear through saved sprite overrides.
- Five new editable prop subjects: mossy masonry, linked tube, central board altar, fern, torch. Native artwork was painted through the actual in-game InlinePixelEditor pencil and Save pixels to rig operations using an isolated scripted authoring session. This was programmatic editor operation, not manual mouse painting. Final active saved part/view count: 151 across seven subjects. Reproduction/export script: `scripts/author-tv-arena-pixels.cjs` (optional `ARENA_SUBJECT` selects one subject).
- Arena rig/pixel data is persisted through ordinary design/project rig packs in a new `arenaRigs` field. The pre-existing shared authored fields were compared against HEAD and preserved. Source-native defaults remain separate; normal saves, house libraries, character profiles and editor drafts are not migrated or replaced.
- Saved prop pixels rasterize once into cropped, bounded surfaces to remove seams during fractional viewport scaling. Forest layers are cached. Live in-memory pixel edits to all five prop subjects are verified to change the actual arena render.
- Seventeen one-use sunseeds; every fifth restores a heart. Existing mushroom blocks, personal inventory, arrow/mana spending, party victory chest and return-to-lobby behavior remain. HUD includes a compact route map and wave/enemy/seed counts; contextual prompts identify travel tubes.
