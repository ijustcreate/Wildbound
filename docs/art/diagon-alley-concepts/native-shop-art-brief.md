# Diagon Alley shop art brief

Use the approved Ollivanders scene as the size and camera reference. New shops keep its 216 × 164 room, 48-unit street opening, ground-anchored furniture, and player-sized doors and counters. Front faces follow the house axes. Roofs follow house projection with distinct ridges and eaves. Draw on the logical world pixel grid. Use cool dark contour and underside values, a broad body plane, light on the upper left, and isolated brass or glass highlights.

- Flourish & Blotts uses deep moss timber, an off-center slate roof and leaded reading windows. The books, lectern and reading lamp define the interior.
- Madam Malkin’s uses cream plaster, a folded aubergine mansard, draped display glass, and a shears emblem. The tailoring form and cloth displays give the room a deliberate fitting area.
- Quality Quidditch Supplies uses a high red timber gable, diamond glazing and a hoop sign. A broom display and repair surfaces bring the workshop forward.
- Slugg & Jiggers uses olive masonry, a copper-capped roof and deep bottle display windows. A still and cauldron sit among tightly grouped ingredients.
- Eeylops uses weathered teal, a shallow divided roof, aviary bars and a carved owl mark. A native perched owl sits among roosts, cages and dry feed.
- Florean Fortescue’s uses faded mint tile, pink canvas, a rounded service bay and a cone sign. The marble counter holds a glass scoop case and small sweets. A bent iron chair and cafe table sit off the main route.
- Gringotts uses pale stone, a triangular pediment with column bays and bronze thresholds. A cool vault door, walnut teller furniture and a wine runner organize a clear customer aisle.

Paint localized warm spill from openings and task lights below props. Keep the cabinet and counter contact shade narrow. Give each merchant a readable silhouette in the stock display. Stage decor off the entrance and service lanes. Evaluate open and closed shops with live player scale and verify navigation from the same coordinates the art uses.
