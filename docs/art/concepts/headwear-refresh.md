# Wildbound headwear studies

These plates cover 30 remaining headwear items and the four corrected Crooked Witch Hats. Each design has three detached angles, a short material brief, and its own 24 × 24 inventory silhouette. The gameplay turnaround shows all eight facings on the same player rig.

- [Travel and festival headwear](headwear-cloth.md)
- [Crowns, helms, circlets, and horns](headwear-crowns.md)
- [Cowls, masks, and halo](headwear-masks.md)
- [Thinner brim and lower fit for the witch hats](headwear-witch-fit.png)

The equipped painter routes through the normal head layer and existing skull pivot. In Item Studio, select an item and change direction before opening its pixel editor. Apply the [Wildbound art workflow](../../../.agents/skills/wildbound-art/SKILL.md) to review at native scale and in motion.
