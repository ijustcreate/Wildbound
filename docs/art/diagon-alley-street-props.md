# Diagon Alley street props

Warner Bros. Studio Tour’s photography shows individual trades spilling into a continuous cobbled street. The broom stand, chained books, folded cloth and kettle cart give adjacent storefronts recognizable goods. The street clock, fingerpost, iron bench, basin and brazier use the same stepped outlines and warm brass highlights as Wildbound’s street lamps. [Diagon Alley set gallery](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/the-experience/explore-the-tour/sets/diagon-alley/) · [Wide set photograph](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/diagon-alley-wide-1920x1080-1.webp)

Universal’s exterior photo shows a business identified from the street by its trade. Inspired by that emphasis, the original caged perch and letterbox sit on the curb by the owl emporium. A herb planter and brass-bound barrel finish the street furnishings. The contacts sit at the foot of each prop, with the central avenue, resident paths and shop approaches available to walk through. [Magical Menagerie exterior](https://www.universalorlando.com/contentdata/uor/pt/br/files/Images/gds/usf-magical-menagerie-shop-exterior-b.jpg)

All 13 sprites compile from editable JSON recipes. The clock hands, basin ripples and brazier flame use timed recipe frames. The kettle steam and brazier spill are anchored to their respective fixtures. The pixel recipe command produced integer-scale previews for all 13. Muted Electron checks cover every prop’s collision, the timed frames, shop access, and shared and split-screen views. Native gameplay captures are under `test-output/diagon-alley/source/street-props-*.png`.
