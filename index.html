A FEW RULES. A LOT OF JUNGLE.

Don't leave anyone behind.

  1. Hit the table to roll. Only the current player's attack wakes the board. Your miniature advances along the trail.
  2. Every roll summons trouble. Fight or trap it together. The next player can roll while creatures are still out.
  3. Stay together. Dodge red attack warnings. Hold Interact beside a fallen friend to revive them.
  4. Call WILDBOUND. Once your miniature reaches the center, hold Interact at the table. The jungle collapses into the board. Loot the shared victory chest, then strike the board to grow a new jungle.
  5. Commit to the adventure. Players may join during the first round. After that the party locks and turns cannot be skipped.
  6. Keep your character. Select a saved explorer in the lobby. Store equipment in your private portal room. Defeat loses carried gear; stored equipment survives.
  7. Fight and loot. Hold attack to charge, release to strike. RT / right mouse dashes. X quick-loots; E / Y interacts or revives. I / Back opens your pack. H / RB drinks a potion. P / D-pad up opens your portal. Q / left-stick click uses a trap. C / LT blocks with a shield.
