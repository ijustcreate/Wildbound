# Wildbound 1.0.26 - Softer light and fog

Launch **Wildbound Latest (1.0.26)** on the Desktop.

The player's visible area now fades gradually through several opacity levels instead of ending in a sharply outlined circle. Unexplored darkness blends across the edges of explored square tiles, with feathering measured in world space so zooming preserves its appearance. Sight radius and exploration rules are unchanged.

Verified with a focused Electron render and pixel falloff check. Previous builds and saves are preserved.
