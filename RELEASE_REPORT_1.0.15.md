# Wildbound 1.0.15 — Cape silhouettes and flutter

Procedural capes now lift and curl substantially higher, with stronger trailing
and lateral flutter. Shoulders remain pinned; movement, dashes and jumps drive
the effect. Custom painted cape attachments retain their existing artwork.

Three new equipable capes, available in the equipment catalog and normal loot:

- Wayfarer’s tattered cape: rare, long silhouette with an uneven torn hem.
- Scout’s half cape: common, half the standard cloth length.
- Swiftwing pointed cape: rare, half-length cloth tapering to a point.

Each has its own color and matching inventory icon. Existing cape items and
custom fitting are preserved. Tests cover silhouette lengths, pinned shoulders,
increased lift and item definitions; visual checks cover rear and side views.
